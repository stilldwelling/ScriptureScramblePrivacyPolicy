Privacy Policy for Scripture Scramble

Last updated: February 3, 2026

Introduction

Scripture Scramble ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Scripture Scramble mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our App.

Information Collection and Use

Scripture Scramble is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information.

Data Stored Locally

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Game progress and statistics
  • Completed puzzle information
  • User preferences and settings
  • Daily puzzle completion status

This data remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or third parties.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services that collect information used to identify you.

Audio Features

The App includes sound effects for gameplay. No audio recording or microphone access is required or used.

Age Requirements

This App is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. All game data is stored locally on the device.

Security

Since we do not collect or transmit any personal data, there are no security concerns related to data transmission. All game data remains securely on your device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
